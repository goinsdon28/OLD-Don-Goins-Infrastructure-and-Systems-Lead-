12 Weeks of Progression

This journal documents a progression from learning a large-scale data-center environment to independent diagnostics, complex ticket ownership, mentoring, and technician training. It is intentionally written as a public portfolio artifact, not as an internal operating guide.

All identifying infrastructure details, internal commands, tool names, ticket identifiers, facility names, and proprietary procedures are removed or generalized.
