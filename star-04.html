STAR Story 04: Operational Reporting Platform

Home STAR Stories Triage Journal Take a Break
Situation

Situation

Operations and support teams needed reliable, understandable production metrics from systems that did not expose the information in a directly usable form.

Task

Task

Build a reporting path that could serve supervisors, engineering, IT, security, HR, and other stakeholders without forcing them to interpret raw system data.

Action

Action

Extracted source data, normalized it in SQL Server, built views and business logic, created SSRS reports, and iterated on output based on how different stakeholders actually used the information.

Result

Result

Delivered a reusable reporting layer that improved visibility and reduced dependence on manual data interpretation.

Interview Focus

SQL Server • SSRS • Stakeholder Reporting
