STAR Story 05: High-Availability Production Printing

Home STAR Stories Triage Journal Take a Break
Situation

Situation

Production printing incidents could stop work at stations and had to be recovered quickly.

Task

Task

Improve printing resilience while keeping troubleshooting practical for recurring P1–P5 incidents.

Action

Action

Diagnosed printer, queue, network, and application paths across Linux/CUPS and Zebra devices, standardized troubleshooting, and built two failover CUPS servers so printing was not dependent on a single service point.

Result

Result

Improved production-printing resilience and created a clearer recovery path during printer and queue failures.

Interview Focus

CUPS • Zebra • Linux • Production Support
