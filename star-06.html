STAR Story 06: VoIP & Paging Integration

Home STAR Stories Triage Journal Take a Break
Situation

Situation

A legacy facility-wide paging system needed to work with a modern cloud PBX environment.

Task

Task

Bridge the new voice platform to existing paging and amplification infrastructure without losing operational coverage.

Action

Action

Configured the cloud PBX paging path, integrated an IP paging adapter, connected it to the existing analog amplification system, validated VLAN/network requirements, tuned audio levels, and tested paging behavior across zones.

Result

Result

Preserved facility paging while modernizing the voice platform and demonstrated integration across network, SIP, and analog systems.
