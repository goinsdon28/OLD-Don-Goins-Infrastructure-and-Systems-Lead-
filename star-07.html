STAR Story 07: Niagara HVAC Controller Recovery

Home STAR Stories Triage Journal Take a Break
Situation

Situation

A lightning event damaged infrastructure, including a building-automation controller used for HVAC visibility and control.

Task

Task

Determine what could be recovered and restore the controller with minimal loss of configuration.

Action

Action

Recovered the available configuration from the failed controller, prepared a replacement unit, restored the configuration, re-established network identity and integrations, and validated that the controller returned to the building-automation dashboard.

Result

Result

Restored HVAC monitoring/control functionality without rebuilding the environment from scratch.
