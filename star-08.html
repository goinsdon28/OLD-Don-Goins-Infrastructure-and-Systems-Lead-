STAR Story 08: Engineering CAD Cost Reduction

Home STAR Stories Triage Journal Take a Break
Situation

Situation

Engineering required CAD capability, but the existing licensing approach carried a high recurring cost.

Task

Task

Provide a workable alternative without disrupting engineering access to the required functionality.

Action

Action

Evaluated a lower-cost CAD platform, installed and configured the server-side components, handled endpoint licensing and access, and supported the transition so engineering could continue working with minimal change to workflow.

Result

Result

Reduced recurring software cost while maintaining needed CAD capability.

Application Delivery • Licensing • Cost Control
