STAR Story 09: Data Center Diagnostic Ownership

Home STAR Stories Triage Journal Take a Break
Situation

Situation

Early in a large-scale data-center role, the challenge was to move from environment familiarity to independently resolving hardware and connectivity tickets.

Task

Task

Build a repeatable triage method and apply it across increasingly complex tickets without guessing or swapping components without evidence.

Action

Action

Used health checks, physical inspection, topology validation, optical/fiber testing, component reseating and replacement only when justified, and clear ticket evidence to progress issues through resolution or appropriate escalation.

Result

Result

Progressed to independent ticket ownership and became someone other technicians could ask for help with the reasoning behind triage.
