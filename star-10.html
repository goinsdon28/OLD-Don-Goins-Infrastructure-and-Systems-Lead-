STAR Story 10: Technician Mentoring & Readiness

Home STAR Stories Triage Journal Take a Break
Situation

Situation

New technicians were learning tools and procedures but often lacked context for when and why to use them.

Task

Task

Help technicians build a reasoning-first troubleshooting process rather than memorize isolated tool actions.

Action

Action

Taught a consistent triage sequence, required evidence before resolution, introduced tools only when the ticket justified them, coached technicians through live work, then stepped back to assess whether they could repeat the process independently.

Result

Result

Multiple technicians closed their own tickets, one escalated appropriately, and the training approach became a practical model for evaluating readiness.

Interview Focus

Mentoring • Training • Technical Leadership
