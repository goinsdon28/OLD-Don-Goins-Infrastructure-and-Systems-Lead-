Week 3 Summary

Moved from isolated actions toward complete triage: understand the symptom, inspect device health, verify physical state, compare expected versus observed behavior, then decide whether to resolve, continue, or escalate.

What Developed This Week

  • ✔ End-to-end triage
  • ✔ Evidence before action
  • ✔ Health/status checks
  • ✔ Resolution criteria

Engineering Reflection

The recurring discipline throughout the journal is to separate symptom from cause, compare expected state to observed state, collect enough evidence to justify the next action, and escalate when the evidence does not support a safe local resolution.
