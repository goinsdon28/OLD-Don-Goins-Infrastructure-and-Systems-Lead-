Week 12 Summary

The final week centered on applying the process to others: formal and informal training, hands-off retention checks, complex ticket ownership, and documenting a reusable readiness curriculum. The goal was no longer just “can I solve it?” but “can I teach someone else to solve it correctly?”

What Developed This Week

  • ✔ Structured training
  • ✔ Hands-off assessment
  • ✔ Complex ticket ownership
  • ✔ Readiness curriculum

Engineering Reflection

The recurring discipline throughout the journal is to separate symptom from cause, compare expected state to observed state, collect enough evidence to justify the next action, and escalate when the evidence does not support a safe local resolution.

Public-Safe Note

This journal entry is intentionally sanitized. Internal tool names, hostnames, ticket identifiers, facility identifiers, company-only procedures, proprietary commands, and confidential architecture details have been removed or generalized.
