Week 13 Summary

13-Week Technical & Leadership Progression

Data Center Technician — Executive Summary

Over my first 13 weeks, my focus has progressed from learning the environment and

becoming independently productive to improving diagnostic quality, mentoring

technicians, and developing repeatable troubleshooting practices that can scale across

the team.


Week 1 — Foundation

Learned site operations, ticket workflow, fiber handling, cleaning, VFL use, and core

diagnostic procedures while beginning independent ticket work.

Week 2 — Technical Independence

Expanded CLI, health-check, fiber-diagnostic, and documentation skills and became

increasingly self-sufficient.

Week 3 — Diagnostic Reasoning

Began correlating telemetry, physical evidence, documentation, and ticket history rather

than relying only on procedural troubleshooting.

Week 4 — Early Mentoring

Maintained personal ticket productivity while helping technicians progress from shadowing

to independently working their own tickets.

Week 5 — Resolution QualityShifted emphasis from ticket volume alone to defensible diagnosis, root-cause

identification, and higher-quality closure documentation.

Week 6 — Team Contribution

Reinforced stronger triage practices during backlog reduction and helped increase

technician confidence and independence.

Week 7 — Advanced Hardware Triage

Diagnosed PSU, PDU, switch, management, loopback, optic, and connectivity issues while

identifying misleading hardware symptoms.

Week 8 — Architecture & Topology

Used expected-versus-discovered topology and system-state verification to safely resolve

complex connectivity issues.

Week 9 — Teaching the Method

Began deliberately teaching health checks, CLI interpretation, anomaly recognition, ticket

documentation, and closure criteria.

Week 10 — Preventing Unnecessary Replacement

Used telemetry and layered troubleshooting to identify true failure points and avoid

unnecessary component replacement and escalation activity.

Week 11 — Technical Leadership

Trained multiple technician groups while maintaining ticket responsibilities and

increasingly became a resource for technical guidance and difficult triage.

Week 12 — Structured Training

Transitioned from informal mentoring to repeatable training focused on diagnostic

reasoning, correct tool selection, evidence gathering, and technician independence.

Week 13 — Measurable Team Impact

Reached 14 notable diagnoses, 10 root causes that simple FRU replacement would have

missed, 5 false closures prevented, 3 incorrect FRU paths stopped before parts

consumption, and 9 evidence-backed escalations. Trained approximately 7–10 technicians

in one day and observed a previously trained technician independently teaching newer

team members.


13-Week Progression: New Technician → Independent Troubleshooter → Advanced Diagnostician → Mentor →

Trainer → Technical Leader

Operating Philosophy:

Diagnose before replacing. Validate automated diagnoses with evidence. Understand the

reason behind the procedure. Teach the reasoning so the knowledge remains with the

team.


My goal isn't simply to become the person everyone comes to for answers; it's to help build

technicians who eventually don't need to come to me for every answer.
